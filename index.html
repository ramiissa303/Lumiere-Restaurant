Our Chef

Cooking is listening to ingredients

Chef Antoine Moreau trained under Alain Passard at L'Arpège and spent three years studying the Japanese philosophy of shun — peak seasonality. He returned to Paris with a conviction: luxury is not excess, but the absolute right ingredient at the absolute right moment.

"Every plate should taste inevitable — as though it could never have been any other way."Antoine Moreau · Executive Chef
