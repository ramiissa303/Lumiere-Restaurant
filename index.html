Our Philosophy

Cooking is
listening to
ingredients.

Chef Antoine Moreau trained under Alain Passard at L'Arpège and spent three years in the Japanese highlands studying the philosophy of shun — the Japanese concept of peak seasonality. He returned to Paris with a conviction: that luxury is not excess, but the absolute right ingredient at the absolute right moment.

"I do not impose flavour. I coax it forward. Every plate should taste inevitable — as though it could never have been any other way."
