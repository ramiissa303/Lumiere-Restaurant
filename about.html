The Beginning

From a six-table bistro

In 2012, Élise Fontaine took over a shuttered six-table bistro on Rue Saint-Dominique with a modest inheritance and an immodest ambition. She had no formal training — only an unshakeable belief that the meals worth remembering are built from honesty, patience, and the very best of what the season offers.

Within four years, Lumière held its first Michelin star. Within seven, its second. Today it is regarded as one of the defining tables in Europe — yet the philosophy has never changed since that first evening service.

The Founder

Élise Fontaine

Élise leads Lumière with a hospitality philosophy borrowed from the great houses of Kyoto: omotenashi — anticipating a guest's needs before they are spoken. She personally greets every table and confirms every reservation.

"Luxury is being seen, remembered, and cared for. Everything else is decoration."Élise Fontaine · Founder
The Chef

Antoine Moreau

Antoine trained under Alain Passard at L'Arpège before spending three years in the Japanese highlands studying shun — the philosophy of peak seasonality. His cooking is defined by restraint: never more than four elements on a plate, each one chosen because nothing else would do.

Our Philosophy

Three principles, unwavering

I.

Season First

The calendar writes our menu. If it is not at its peak this week, it does not reach your table.

II.

Provenance

We know every grower, fisher and forager by name. Trust is our only certification.

III.

Restraint

A Journey

The Lumière Timeline

2012

The Doors Open

Élise Fontaine opens a six-table bistro on Rue Saint-Dominique, cooking a single daily menu.

2014

Antoine Joins

Antoine Moreau returns from Japan and takes the pass. The tasting-menu format is born.

2016

First Michelin Star

The Guide awards Lumière its first star, praising its "startling seasonal clarity."

2019

Second Star

A second star follows, alongside Gault & Millau's Chef of the Year for Antoine.

2022

The Cellar

Our subterranean cellar expands to 900 references, curated by head sommelier Marguerite Vidal.

2024

World's 50 Best

Lumière enters the World's 50 Best Restaurants at No. 14 — its highest honour yet.
